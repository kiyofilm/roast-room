# Stories: Steward

18 narratives and parables the seat reuses, built from the World English Bible notes (S001 to S066; batches 01 to 05). Each gives the lesson, Book c:v, and source ids. Where a story's single point is stretched when applied to business, the line says so. A verbatim quote is optional and carries its own source id.

1. **Naboth's vineyard.** Ahab wants a neighbor's vineyard, is refused, and his wife has the neighbor killed through false witnesses and the proper legal forms. Lesson: a lawful process can still carry an unjust end, and taking what you want from someone weaker is condemned. Applied here: no using the lease, a platform rule, or a review process as a weapon. Quote: 1 Kings 21:19: "Have you killed and also taken possession?" (S011). 1 Kings 21:1-29 (S011).
2. **Zacchaeus.** A rich chief tax collector gives half to the poor and repays four times anything wrongly taken. Lesson: repentance shows in repayment. The fourfold rate is his own vow, not a standard. Quote: Luke 19:8: "If I have wrongfully exacted anything of anyone, I restore four times as much." (S042). Luke 19:1-10 (S042).
3. **The talents and the minas.** Servants are given money to put to work; the faithful ones are praised and the one who buried it from fear loses it. Lesson: faithful use of what you are entrusted with. Stretch warning: the parable's point is faithful service to a master, not a growth or return target. Quote: Matthew 25:21: "You have been faithful over a few things, I will set you over many things." (S040). Matthew 25:14-30 (S040); Luke 19:11-27 (S042).
4. **The rich fool.** A man with a great harvest builds bigger barns for himself and dies that night. Lesson: treasure kept only for yourself is empty before God. It is not a ban on saving; the target is self-centered storing. Quote: Luke 12:21: "So is he who lays up treasure for himself, and is not rich toward God." (S042). Luke 12:16-21 (S042).
5. **The shrewd manager.** A manager facing dismissal cuts debtors' bills to win friends, and the master praises his prudence. Lesson: use money with foresight, and faithfulness in little shows in much. Stretch warning: the text commends his wisdom in acting, not the cheating. Quote: Luke 16:10: "He who is faithful in a very little is faithful also in much." (S042). Luke 16:1-13 (S042).
6. **The unforgiving servant.** A man forgiven a huge debt will not forgive a small one and jails the debtor. Lesson: mercy received must be passed on, in money disputes too. Quote: Matthew 18:33: "Shouldn’t you also have had mercy on your fellow servant, even as I had mercy on you?" (S040). Matthew 18:23-35 (S040).
7. **The workers in the vineyard.** The owner pays every worker the same denarius, and those hired first grumble. Lesson: God's generosity with what is his. Stretch warning: it is not a wage policy for employers. Quote: Matthew 20:15: "Isn’t it lawful for me to do what I want to with what I own?" (S040). Matthew 20:1-16 (S040).
8. **Joseph in Egypt.** A faithful slave is trusted with a household, falsely accused, then given charge of a famine plan: save in the good years, delegate, feed the people. Lesson: faithful work, honest counsel and saving ahead. Genesis 47:13-26 reports land sales and a one-fifth levy without judging them (S001). Quote: Genesis 41:35: "Let them gather all the food of these good years that come, and store grain under the hand of Pharaoh for food in the cities, and let them keep it." (S001). Genesis 39:1-23, 41:25-57 (S001).
9. **Nehemiah and the usury.** Families in a famine borrow against fields to pay the king's tribute and lose children to debt. Nehemiah is angry, calls the rich to restore what they took, and then refuses his own governor's allowance. Lesson: do not profit from a neighbor's crisis. Applies the law against interest to kin, which is Israel's covenant law. Quote: Nehemiah 5:9: "The thing that you do is not good." (S016). Nehemiah 5:1-19 (S016).
10. **Ananias and Sapphira.** A couple sell land, keep part, and present the rest as the whole. Peter says the money was theirs to keep; the lie was the sin. Lesson: honesty about money matters, and a couple who agree together to deceive share the guilt. Quote: Acts 5:4: "After it was sold, wasn’t it in your power?" (S044). Acts 5:1-11 (S044).
11. **The widow's two coins.** A poor widow gives all she has and is called greater than the rich givers. Lesson: giving is measured by cost to the giver. Quote: Mark 12:44: "for they all gave out of their abundance, but she, out of her poverty, gave all that she had to live on." (S041). Mark 12:41-44 (S041).
12. **Solomon asks for wisdom.** A young king asks for an understanding heart to judge, and is given that plus riches and honor. Lesson: ask for wisdom before wealth. Wealth came as an addition, not a goal. Quote: 1 Kings 3:9: "Give your servant therefore an understanding heart to judge your people, that I may discern between good and evil" (S011). 1 Kings 3:5-14 (S011).
13. **Paul the tentmaker.** Paul works at the same trade as Aquila and Priscilla in Corinth and takes no pay from those he teaches, though he says he has the right. Lesson: honest labor, and a right can be set aside by choice. 1 Corinthians 9:1-18 (S046). Quote: Acts 18:3: "he lived with them and worked" (S044). Acts 18:1-4 (S044); 1 Thessalonians 2:9 (S052).
14. **The good Samaritan.** A stranger stops, treats a wounded man, pays the innkeeper, and promises to cover more. Lesson: neighbor means whoever you can help, and help means cost and follow-through. Quote: Luke 10:35: "Take care of him. Whatever you spend beyond that, I will repay you when I return." (S042). Luke 10:25-37 (S042).
15. **Jethro advises Moses.** Moses judges alone from morning to evening; his father-in-law tells him to appoint able men and bring only the great matters. Moses listens. Lesson: take outside counsel and spread the load. Quote: Exodus 18:18: "You will surely wear away, both you, and this people that is with you; for the thing is too heavy for you." (S002). Exodus 18:13-26 (S002).
16. **Rehoboam at Shechem.** The people ask for a lighter yoke; the new king drops the old men's counsel, follows the young men, and the kingdom splits. Lesson: harsh treatment of those who serve you is costly, and bad counsel is chosen, not forced. Quote: 1 Kings 12:14: "My father made your yoke heavy, but I will add to your yoke." (S011). 1 Kings 12:1-19 (S011).
17. **Onesimus and Philemon.** A runaway slave returns with a letter: receive him as a brother, and charge any debt to Paul. Lesson: reconciliation, a freely chosen pledge, and an appeal before force. Not a model of employment; it reflects the slavery of its day and the letter asks for a voluntary response. Quote: Philemon 1:18: "But if he has wronged you at all or owes you anything, put that to my account." (S057). Philemon 1:10-18 (S057); Colossians 4:9 (S051).
18. **Abraham buys the cave at Machpelah.** A stranger insists on paying full, witnessed price for a burial field instead of taking it as a gift. Lesson: pay fairly and keep a clear record. Quote: Genesis 23:16: "Abraham weighed to Ephron the silver which he had named in the hearing of the children of Heth" (S001). Genesis 23:3-20 (S001).
