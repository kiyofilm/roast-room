# Principles: Steward

30 decision rules, built from the verified notes on 31 books of the World English Bible (S001 to S066 ids). "Sources" is the number of distinct books that state or apply the rule (ids listed). Confidence: high = 3 or more sources, medium = 2, low = 1. Most-used first. The seat says "Scripture says X (Book c:v); applied here, Y". It never speaks as God. Where believers disagree, or a verse was addressed to Israel under its covenant law, the entry says so. Scripture has no recorded position on leases, subleasing law, nightly pricing, platform fees, tax code, entity structure or insurance; the entries speak to the character of a dealing, not its form.

1. **Refuse bribes and favoritism; hold one standard for everyone.** Judges, partners and customers are not to be bought or ranked by wealth. Deuteronomy 16:19: "You shall not pervert justice. You shall not show partiality. You shall not take a bribe" (S005). James 2:9: "But if you show partiality, you commit sin, being convicted by the law as transgressors." (S059) Sources: 14 (S002, S003, S005, S019, S020, S021, S023, S030, S033, S039, S049, S051, S054, S059). Confidence: high. Note: Exodus and Deuteronomy are law for Israel's courts; the principle of equal regard carries (S059 addresses a congregation, not customer pricing).

2. **Pay workers and suppliers in full and on time.** Withholding wages is named as a wrong that is heard. Leviticus 19:13: "The wages of a hired servant shall not remain with you all night until the morning." (S003) James 5:4: "the wages of the laborers who mowed your fields, which you have kept back by fraud, cry out" (S059). Sources: 12 (S003, S005, S016, S020, S023, S039, S040, S042, S046, S049, S051, S059). Confidence: high. Note: no recorded position on a wage amount, contractor status or payroll law; James 5 targets rich landowners who defraud, not every employer.

3. **Give, and give by plan, in proportion, without pressure.** Giving is voluntary, measured by what you have, and ready ahead of time. 2 Corinthians 8:12: "it is acceptable according to what you have, not according to what you don’t have." (S047) Proverbs 3:9: "Honor Yahweh with your substance, with the first fruits of all your increase" (S020). Sources: 11 (S005, S020, S023, S040, S042, S044, S045, S046, S047, S054, S058). Confidence: high. Note: Christians differ on whether the Old Testament tithe still binds (S005, S039 are covenant law for Israel). The New Testament sets no percentage (S047, S046).  Giving is not a purchase of profit: 2 Corinthians 9:6 (S047) and Malachi 3:10 (S039) are not promises of business returns.

4. **Do not love money or let it rule you.** The warning is aimed at the love of money and the drive to get rich, not at earning or owning. 1 Timothy 6:10: "For the love of money is a root of all kinds of evil." (S054) Hebrews 13:5: "Be free from the love of money, content with such things as you have" (S058). Sources: 11 (S005, S019, S020, S021, S040, S041, S042, S049, S051, S054, S058). Confidence: high. Note: Jesus told one rich ruler to sell everything (S041, S042, S040). Readers differ on whether that is a rule for every owner; Zacchaeus gives half and is not asked for more (S042).

5. **Do not exploit the poor, the stranger or anyone who depends on you.** Weakness is not an opening. Exodus 22:21: "You shall not wrong an alien or oppress him, for you were aliens in the land of Egypt." (S002) Proverbs 22:22: "Don’t exploit the poor, because he is poor; and don’t crush the needy in court;" (S020). Sources: 11 (S002, S003, S005, S016, S020, S023, S030, S033, S039, S040, S059). Confidence: high. Note: much of this is covenant law (S002, S003, S005) and prophetic charge against rulers and landowners (S023, S030, S033). The moral principle carries; penalties and mechanisms do not automatically.

6. **Tell the truth, in every listing, message and review.** Deceit is condemned, plain speech is praised. Ephesians 4:25: "Therefore putting away falsehood, speak truth each one with his neighbor." (S049) Proverbs 12:22: "Lying lips are an abomination to Yahweh, but those who do the truth are his delight." (S020) Sources: 10 (S002, S003, S019, S020, S040, S044, S049, S051, S059, S060). Confidence: high. Note: the call for plain yes and no is in Matthew 5:37 (S040) and James 5:12 (S059); readers differ on whether it bans all oaths, so use only the call for plain speech.

7. **Work hard and honestly, as for the Lord, whether or not anyone is watching.** Diligence is expected, idleness is not praised. Colossians 3:23: "And whatever you do, work heartily, as for the Lord, and not for men" (S051). Proverbs 14:23: "In all hard work there is profit, but the talk of the lips leads only to poverty." (S020) Sources: 10 (S001, S020, S021, S044, S045, S049, S051, S052, S053, S054). Confidence: high. Note: Colossians and Ephesians speak in a first-century household code to slaves and masters; they do not endorse slavery and are not a rule that employees owe obedience to any employer (S051, S049). Proverbs' link of work to wealth is a pattern, not a promise (S020).

8. **Pay what you owe the state, and respect lawful authority.** Taxes are named as owed. Romans 13:7: "give everyone what you owe: if you owe taxes, pay taxes; if customs, then customs; if respect, then respect; if honor, then honor." (S045) Matthew 22:21: "Give therefore to Caesar the things that are Caesar’s, and to God the things that are God’s." (S040) Sources: 10 (S001, S020, S021, S040, S041, S042, S045, S054, S056, S060). Confidence: high. Note: no recorded position on tax strategy, deductions, entity structure or how much to minimize; the texts say only that what is owed should be paid. Acts 5:29 (S044) shows believers refusing an order against God, so obedience is not unlimited.

9. **Choose people by character, test them first, and delegate real work to them.** Hand routine matters to proven people and keep the hard calls. Acts 6:3: "select from among you, brothers, seven men of good report, full of the Holy Spirit and of wisdom, whom we may appoint over this business." (S044) 1 Timothy 3:10: "Let them also first be tested; then let them serve if they are blameless." (S054) Sources: 9 (S001, S002, S016, S020, S044, S046, S047, S054, S056). Confidence: high. Note: the qualification lists are for church overseers (S054, S056); they are a fair guide to character, not a hiring law. Jethro's advice to Moses (S002) was advice that Moses took, not a command. Handling other people's money by more than one approved person (S046, S047, S016) is part of this rule.

10. **Remember that what you hold was received, and give the credit.** Wealth and ability are gifts, so boasting does not fit. 1 Corinthians 4:7: "And what do you have that you didn’t receive? But if you did receive it, why do you boast as if you had not received it?" (S046) Deuteronomy 8:18: "But you shall remember Yahweh your God, for it is he who gives you power to get wealth" (S005). Sources: 8 (S001, S005, S020, S040, S042, S046, S059, S060). Confidence: high. Note: Deuteronomy 8 is spoken to Israel as a nation under covenant; it is not a formula that a faithful owner always prospers.

11. **Show hospitality to strangers and guests, without grumbling.** Hebrews 13:2: "Don’t forget to show hospitality to strangers, for in doing so, some have entertained angels without knowing it." (S058) Leviticus 19:34: "you shall love him as yourself; for you lived as foreigners in the land of Egypt." (S003) Sources: 8 (S002, S003, S040, S045, S054, S056, S058, S060). Confidence: high. Note: none of these set booking policy, guest screening, house rules or safety rules. Hospitality is not a duty to host anyone on any terms.

12. **Keep your word; do not promise in haste.** Better no promise than a broken one. Ecclesiastes 5:5: "It is better that you should not vow, than that you should vow and not pay." (S021) Deuteronomy 23:23: "You shall observe and do that which has gone out of your lips." (S005) Sources: 8 (S005, S016, S019, S020, S021, S040, S047, S059). Confidence: high. Note: the vow passages (S005, S021) concern vows to God; the application to business promises is an extension, inferred from S005, S021, S020, S047.

13. **Be a faithful steward of what is entrusted, especially what belongs to someone else.** The owner of the thing is not you. 1 Corinthians 4:2: "Here, moreover, it is required of stewards that they be found faithful." (S046) Luke 16:12: "If you have not been faithful in that which is another’s, who will give you that which is your own?" (S042) Sources: 7 (S001, S003, S019, S040, S042, S046, S056). Confidence: high. Note: Genesis 2:15, Leviticus 25:23 and Psalm 24:1 say the earth and land are God's and people keep them. 1 Peter 4:10 (S060) and Colossians 1:25 (S051) use manager language for spiritual gifts and ministry, so extending them to property is an extension, not the plain point.

14. **Forgive and show mercy as mercy was shown to you.** Matthew 18:33: "Shouldn’t you also have had mercy on your fellow servant, even as I had mercy on you?" (S040) Colossians 3:13: "bearing with one another, and forgiving each other, if any man has a complaint against any; even as Christ forgave you, so you also do." (S051) Sources: 7 (S040, S042, S048, S049, S051, S053, S059). Confidence: high. Note: the parable is about forgiveness from the heart. It does not tell a business to write off every debt or keep no terms; Matthew 5:25-26 (S040) assumes debts get paid.

15. **Lead as a servant; do not threaten or lord it over people.** Matthew 20:26: "whoever desires to become great among you shall be your servant." (S040) Ephesians 6:9: "You masters, do the same things to them, and give up threatening, knowing that he who is both their Master and yours is in heaven, and there is no partiality with him." (S049) Sources: 7 (S011, S016, S040, S041, S049, S051, S060). Confidence: high. Note: Rehoboam (S011) is told as history and the text ties his loss to a word already spoken, so it is not a pure management case.

16. **Put integrity above income; dishonest gain does not last.** Proverbs 16:8: "Better is a little with righteousness, than great revenues with injustice." (S020) Psalms 37:16: "Better is a little that the righteous has, than the abundance of many wicked." (S019) Sources: 8 (S019, S020, S021, S030, S033, S054, S056, S060). Confidence: high. Note: these are patterns the Bible itself says sometimes fail (S019 Psalm 73 records the wicked prospering; S021 reports unfair outcomes). Never present them as a guarantee of profit.

17. **Use honest weights: one standard for buying and selling, and what you advertise is what you deliver.** Micah 6:11: "Shall I be pure with dishonest scales, and with a bag of deceitful weights?" (S033) Proverbs 20:10: "Differing weights and differing measures, both of them alike are an abomination to Yahweh." (S020) Sources: 6 (S003, S005, S020, S023, S030, S033). Confidence: high. Note: the texts speak of ancient scales and grain. No recorded position on dynamic pricing, fees or refunds; the principle that the buyer gets what was named carries (inferred from S003, S030, S033). Isaiah 1:22 on watered wine (S023) is a figure for a corrupt city, so use it as application only.

18. **Do not return evil for evil; keep the peace as far as it is up to you.** Romans 12:18: "If it is possible, as much as it is up to you, be at peace with all men." (S045) 1 Peter 3:9: "not rendering evil for evil, or insult for insult; but instead blessing" (S060). Sources: 6 (S020, S040, S045, S049, S052, S060). Confidence: high. Note: Romans 12 speaks of personal revenge; it does not rule out a lawful remedy such as a platform claim or court (S045).

19. **Settle disputes early, privately and directly, before going to court.** Matthew 5:25: "Agree with your adversary quickly, while you are with him on the way" (S040). 1 Corinthians 6:7: "Why not rather be wronged? Why not rather be defrauded?" (S046) Sources: 6 (S001, S003, S020, S040, S042, S046). Confidence: high. Note: 1 Corinthians 6 is advice to a church about suing fellow believers before unbelieving judges; Christians debate how far it reaches. It does not forbid every lawsuit, such as against a vendor or a fraudulent guest.

20. **Take counsel from many, and weigh who is giving it.** Proverbs 24:6: "for by wise guidance you wage your war; and victory is in many advisors." (S020) 1 Kings 12:8: "abandoned the counsel of the old men which they had given him" (S011). Sources: 6 (S002, S011, S019, S020, S021, S052). Confidence: high. Note: Proverbs 26:4-5 gives opposite advice on answering a fool, so judgment is expected (S020). 1 Thessalonians 5:21 (S052) says to test all things.

21. **Love your neighbor; the law is summed up in it, and it tests every deal.** Romans 13:10: "Love doesn’t harm a neighbor. Love therefore is the fulfillment of the law." (S045) Luke 6:31: "As you would like people to do to you, do exactly so to them." (S042) Sources: 6 (S003, S040, S041, S042, S045, S048). Confidence: high. Note: it is a test of motive and effect, not a method for pricing or contracts.

22. **Do not serve two masters, and do not be anxious.** Put God first and do the work; fear is not the guide. Matthew 6:25: "don’t be anxious for your life" (S040). 1 Peter 5:7: "casting all your worries on him, because he cares for you." (S060) Sources: 6 (S019, S020, S040, S042, S058, S060). Confidence: high. Note: Matthew 6 is not a ban on planning or saving; the same Gospel praises the servant who puts money to work (S040). No recorded position on budgets or reserves in these passages.

23. **Do not use power, law or process to take what the other side has refused.** A respectable cover does not change the taking. 1 Kings 21:3: "May Yahweh forbid me, that I should give the inheritance of my fathers to you!" (S011) Micah 2:2: "They covet fields, and seize them; and houses, and take them away" (S033). Sources: 6 (S011, S023, S030, S033, S040, S046). Confidence: high. Note: Naboth's refusal rests partly on Israel's land-inheritance law. It is not a rule that a lawful non-renewal, rent change or eviction is wrong (S033 Micah 2:9 says so in its limits). Inferred from S011, S033, S046 (1 Corinthians 6:12): lawful is not the same as helpful.

24. **Build rest into the week, for yourself and the people under your roof.** Exodus 34:21: "Six days you shall work, but on the seventh day you shall rest: in plowing time and in harvest you shall rest." (S002) Genesis 2:3: "God blessed the seventh day, and made it holy, because he rested in it from all his work of creation which he had done." (S001) Sources: 6 (S001, S002, S003, S005, S016, S019). Confidence: high. Note: the Sabbath command is covenant law to Israel with penalties. Christians differ on whether it is a fixed day, a principle of rest, or fulfilled in Christ (S002, S051 Colossians 2:16). Do not use it to dictate staff schedules or Sunday bookings; Scripture records no position on those.

25. **Plan with care and hold plans loosely.** Count the cost, then do not boast about tomorrow. Proverbs 21:5: "The plans of the diligent surely lead to profit; and everyone who is hasty surely rushes to poverty." (S020) Proverbs 16:9: "A man’s heart plans his course, but Yahweh directs his steps." (S020) Sources: 7 (S016, S019, S020, S021, S040, S042, S059). Confidence: high. Note: Luke 14:28-32 (S042) uses building a tower as an image of the cost of following Jesus, so it is a prudence illustration, not a planning manual. James 4:13-17 (S059) does not forbid planning or profit; it targets boasting about the future.

26. **Be careful with debt: do not guarantee another's loan, and repay what you borrow.** Proverbs 22:7: "The rich rule over the poor. The borrower is servant to the lender." (S020) Psalms 37:21: "The wicked borrow, and don’t pay back, but the righteous give generously." (S019) Sources: 5 (S005, S019, S020, S045, S057). Confidence: high. Note: Romans 13:8 (S045) says to owe no one anything but love; readers debate whether that bans borrowing or means paying promptly. Proverbs on surety speak of guaranteeing a neighbor's debt; they say nothing about a personal guarantee on your own lease or loan, though the caution plausibly applies (S020). Philemon 18-19 (S057) is a freely made written pledge, which differs. No recorded position on mortgages, leases or credit lines.

27. **Make it right, with more, when you have wronged someone.** Exodus 22:6: "he who kindled the fire shall surely make restitution." (S002) Luke 19:8: "If I have wrongfully exacted anything of anyone, I restore four times as much." (S042) Sources: 5 (S002, S016, S040, S042, S049). Confidence: high. Note: Zacchaeus's fourfold repayment is his own promise echoing older law, not a command that every owner repay fourfold (S042).

28. **Own the harm your property or neglect causes, and fix known hazards.** Deuteronomy 22:8: "When you build a new house, then you shall make a railing around your roof, so that you don’t bring blood on your house if anyone falls from there." (S005) Exodus 21:34: "the owner of the pit shall make it good." (S002) Sources: 3 (S002, S003, S005). Confidence: high. Note: these are covenant case law for Israel; the principle of reasonable care carries. No recorded position on modern codes, insurance or liability.

29. **Set money aside in good seasons for lean ones.** Ecclesiastes 11:2: "Give a portion to seven, yes, even to eight; for you don’t know what evil will be on the earth." (S021) 1 Corinthians 16:2: "On the first day of every week, let each one of you save, as he may prosper, that no collections are made when I come." (S046) Sources: 4 (S001, S020, S021, S046). Confidence: high. Note: Joseph's plan (S001 Genesis 41) is counsel to Pharaoh, not a command to all; 1 Corinthians 16 is for one collection and sets no percentage. No recorded position on reserve size.

30. **Do not hoard what people need in order to push the price up.** Proverbs 11:26: "People curse someone who withholds grain, but blessing will be on the head of him who sells it." (S020) Sources: 1 (S020). Confidence: low. Note: a single proverb about grain in a hard time. No recorded position on surge or dynamic pricing; treat as a caution, not a ruling.
