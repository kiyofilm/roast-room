# Books: Steward

31 of the 66 books of the World English Bible were mined (S001 to S066 in sources.md); the other 35 were not. They would add these themes: Ruth (gleaning, redeeming a relative, a land purchase done in public), Job (suffering that does not follow a formula), Jeremiah and Ezekiel (more on unjust building, wages, extortion and personal responsibility), Daniel and Esther (working under a hostile or foreign authority), the historical books (Joshua to 2 Kings and Chronicles, Ezra: more cases of kings, land and budgets), Song of Solomon (no business bearing), the minor prophets not read (Hosea, Joel, Obadiah, Jonah, Nahum, Habakkuk, Zephaniah, Haggai, Zechariah), John, Philippians (contentment), 2 Timothy, 2 Peter, 1 to 3 John, Jude and Revelation. The seat should not claim these as sources. Where the 31 books are silent, say "no recorded position".

Entries are grouped by genre. Each gives what the book teaches a business owner, genre, how the seat should use it, and the passages used. No book text is reproduced.

## Law and story (Genesis to Deuteronomy)

### Genesis (S001)
- Genre: narrative, mostly description, given before Israel's covenant law.
- Teaches: work and keeping what is entrusted; rest; saving in plenty; paying a named price openly; yielding a point to keep peace; faithful service to another's goods.
- Seat use: cite as example, not command. Do not turn Joseph's land deals into a rule.
- Passages: 1:28, 2:3, 2:15, 3:19, 13:8-13, 23:3-20, 39:1-23, 41:25-57, 47:13-26.

### Exodus (S002)
- Genre: narrative and covenant law to Israel, with Jethro's advice as counsel.
- Teaches: delegation and counsel; Sabbath rest for all; owning harm your property causes; restitution; no wronging the stranger, widow or orphan; no bribes or false reports; collateral returned by night.
- Seat use: carry the moral principle, not the penalty or court. Interest and Sabbath rules are covenant law debated for Christians.
- Passages: 18:13-26, 20:8-17, 21:29-36, 22:1-27, 23:1-12, 31:1-6, 34:21.

### Leviticus (S003)
- Genre: law to Israel, holiness code.
- Teaches: gleaning margins; pay wages the same day; honest scales; do not hide a hazard; rebuke directly; price by what is actually sold; God owns the land.
- Seat use: lasting moral commands sit beside ceremonial ones; say where the line is debated. Leviticus 27 was not read.
- Passages: 19:9-18, 19:33-37, 23:22, 25:14-43.

### Deuteronomy (S005)
- Genre: Moses's speech restating law to Israel.
- Teaches: rest for everyone under your roof; wealth is a gift and success tempts pride; same-day wages; no collateral that takes a livelihood; honest weights; fix roof hazards; keep vows.
- Seat use: cite the principle, not the seven-year release or interest rule, which concern fellow Israelites. Chapter 28 was read only briefly.
- Passages: 5:12-21, 8:10-18, 14:22-29, 15:1-15, 16:17-20, 22:1-8, 23:19-25, 24:6-22, 25:13-16.

## History and wisdom

### 1 Kings (S011)
- Genre: history narrative of kings.
- Teaches: ask for wisdom; serve those under you; do not trade seasoned counsel for flatterers; Naboth's vineyard: a lawful cover does not make a taking right.
- Seat use: description, not procedure. Rehoboam's loss is also tied to a word already spoken.
- Passages: 3:5-28, 12:1-19, 21:1-29.

### Nehemiah (S016)
- Genre: first-person memoir of a governor and builder.
- Teaches: inspect before announcing; pray and post a guard; restore what was taken from debtors; refuse pay you could claim; faithful treasurers; pay the workers.
- Seat use: his own restraint is his choice, not a command. The usury rebuke concerned a famine among Jews, not modern credit. Do not use his force in chapter 13 as a model.
- Passages: 2:1-18, 4:1-23, 5:1-19, 13:4-22.

### Psalms (S019)
- Genre: prayer, praise and wisdom poetry. Only Psalms 1, 15, 24, 37, 49, 50, 62, 73, 112, 127, 128 were read.
- Teaches: integrity and a kept oath; everything belongs to God; do not envy the wicked; honest poverty beats crooked wealth; generous lending; effort without God is empty.
- Seat use: general patterns, not promises. Psalm 73 shows the wicked prospering.
- Passages: 1:1-3, 15, 24:1-4, 37:1-25, 49, 50:8-21, 62, 73, 112, 127, 128.

### Proverbs (S020)
- Genre: wisdom sayings, a father to a son.
- Teaches: honest weights; pay when due; avoid surety; many counselors; diligence; dishonest gain dwindles; do not exploit the poor; slow anger; know the state of your flocks.
- Seat use: patterns of how life usually goes, not guarantees. Discipline verses concern children, not staff or guests.
- Passages: 3:9, 3:27-29, 6:1-11, 10:4, 11:1, 11:15, 11:26, 12:22, 13:11, 14:23, 15:22, 16:8-9, 20:10, 21:5, 22:1-7, 22:22, 24:6, 24:27, 27:23.

### Ecclesiastes (S021)
- Genre: reflective first-person observation.
- Teaches: money never satisfies; keep vows; spread risk and keep sowing; two are better than one; enjoy work as a gift; fear God.
- Seat use: the Preacher reports how life looks; he does not promise reward. Do not read verses on enjoyment as permission for waste.
- Passages: 2:1-11, 3:13, 4:4-12, 5:2-15, 7:7-12, 9:10-11, 10:10, 10:18, 11:2-6, 12:13-14.

## Prophets

### Isaiah (S023)
- Genre: prophetic oracle to Judah. Only chapters 1, 5 and 58 were read.
- Teaches: worship without justice is rejected; woe to those who buy up house and field until no room is left; bribes; true devotion frees, feeds and houses; do not oppress laborers while fasting.
- Seat use: aimed at a nation's rulers and landowners under covenant; not a rule against growth.
- Passages: 1:11-23, 5:1-23, 58:1-13.

### Amos (S030)
- Genre: prophetic oracle against Israel's upper class.
- Teaches: the poor are not for sale; clothes taken in pledge are not for comfort; false scales and sweepings sold with the wheat; justice over religious show.
- Seat use: a judgment on a nation, not a business manual and not permission to punish others.
- Passages: 2:6-8, 3:9-10, 4:1, 5:10-24, 6:1-6, 7:10-15, 8:4-6.

### Micah (S033)
- Genre: prophetic covenant lawsuit.
- Teaches: act justly, love mercy, walk humbly; do not seize fields and houses because it is in the power of your hand; dishonest scales; statutes can serve an unjust cause.
- Seat use: Micah 6:8 is a summary, not a method for hard cases. Micah 2:9 does not address a lawful non-renewal.
- Passages: 2:1-9, 3:9-12, 6:1-16, 7:3-7.

### Malachi (S039)
- Genre: prophetic dialogue to Israel's priests and people.
- Teaches: give your best, not leftovers; do not deal treacherously; God is a witness against those who cheat the hired worker; tithes (covenant).
- Seat use: resist the prosperity reading of Malachi 3:10; it was a promise to a nation, and the book itself records the proud doing well.
- Passages: 1:6-14, 2:5-10, 3:5-10, 3:13-18.

## Gospels

### Matthew (S040)
- Genre: gospel; Jesus's teaching and parables.
- Teaches: treasure and heart; two masters; do not be anxious; reconcile first; go to the person first; the unforgiving servant; faithful with what is entrusted; render to Caesar; golden rule.
- Seat use: each parable makes one point; the talents and vineyard parables are not wage or interest policy. Matthew 23 is aimed at religious leaders.
- Passages: 5:23-42, 6:1-34, 7:12, 17:24-27, 18:15-35, 19:16-26, 20:1-28, 22:15-22, 23:1-26, 25:14-46.

### Mark (S041)
- Genre: gospel.
- Teaches: do not defraud; trusting in riches is the problem; servant leadership; render to Caesar; the widow's gift.
- Seat use: Mark 10:21 is a personal call to one man. The vineyard tenants parable is not a lease rule.
- Passages: 10:17-27, 10:42-45, 12:1-17, 12:28-44.

### Luke (S042)
- Genre: gospel with many parables on money.
- Teaches: collect only what is due; be content with wages; the rich fool; the faithful steward; count the cost; faithful in little; two masters; Zacchaeus repays; the minas.
- Seat use: the shrewd manager is praised for prudence, not the dishonest act. Zacchaeus's repayment is his own promise.
- Passages: 3:8-14, 6:31-38, 10:7, 10:25-37, 12:13-48, 14:12-33, 16:1-31, 18:9-25, 19:1-27.

## History of the early church

### Acts (S044)
- Genre: narrative of the early church.
- Teaches: honesty about money (Ananias and Sapphira); delegate and fix unfairness (the seven); work with your hands and help the weak; obey God over men when they conflict.
- Seat use: Acts 2 and 4 are description, not a rule to hold things in common. Acts 5:29 is not a license to ignore any law.
- Passages: 2:45-46, 4:32-37, 5:1-11, 5:29, 6:1-7, 18:1-4, 18:24-28, 20:17-38.

## Letters of Paul and others

### Romans (S045)
- Genre: letter; teaching then short commands.
- Teaches: pay taxes and what is owed; owe nothing but love; love does not harm a neighbor; do not repay evil for evil; do not judge on disputable matters.
- Seat use: pay what is owed; the text is silent on tax strategy. Romans 13:8 is debated on borrowing.
- Passages: 12:3-21, 13:1-10, 14:1-23, 15:1, 15:25-27.

### 1 Corinthians (S046)
- Genre: letter to a church with problems.
- Teaches: stewards must be faithful; everything you have was received; settle disputes wisely, better wronged than a suit among believers; lawful is not helpful; workers share the fruit; save weekly; accountable handling.
- Seat use: All things are lawful is a slogan Paul quotes and qualifies. Chapter 6 does not ban every lawsuit.
- Passages: 4:1-12, 6:1-12, 9:7-18, 10:23-33, 16:1-4, 16:14.

### 2 Corinthians (S047)
- Genre: letter; appeal about a collection. Chapters 8 and 9 were read; chapter 12 was not.
- Teaches: give from what you have, cheerfully, by plan, and finish; handle money above suspicion.
- Seat use: not a tithing rule. Sow and reap is not a promise of profit.
- Passages: 8:1-21, 9:5-15.

### Galatians (S048)
- Genre: letter arguing against requiring the Law of Moses.
- Teaches: use freedom to serve; love your neighbor; bear one another's burdens and your own load; do not tire of doing good; remember the poor.
- Seat use: verses on the law are not advice on modern rules. Galatians 6:7 is not a promise that giving returns money.
- Passages: 2:10, 5:13-26, 6:1-10.

### Ephesians (S049)
- Genre: letter; doctrine then practical commands.
- Teaches: stop stealing and work to give; speak truth; do not let anger run overnight; masters give up threats; covetousness is out of place.
- Seat use: the household code is first-century and does not endorse slavery; the command to masters carries.
- Passages: 4:25-32, 5:3-16, 6:4-9.

### Colossians (S051)
- Genre: letter; doctrine then daily practice.
- Teaches: work heartily as for the Lord; no eye-service; masters give what is just and equal; do not lie; forgive; covetousness is idolatry.
- Seat use: same household-code caution. Colossians 2:16 is not a work-hours rule. Colossians 1:25 uses stewardship for ministry, not property.
- Passages: 3:5-25, 4:1-6.

### 1 Thessalonians (S052)
- Genre: warm letter to a young church.
- Teaches: quiet life, your own business, own hands; no flattery or cloak for greed; do not repay evil for evil; test all things.
- Seat use: 4:6 sits in a sexual-conduct passage; do not rest a business rule on it alone. End-times lines are not risk advice.
- Passages: 2:5-9, 4:9-12, 5:11-22.

### 2 Thessalonians (S053)
- Genre: letter; command and comfort.
- Teaches: if anyone will not work, do not let him eat; Paul paid his own way; admonish as a brother.
- Seat use: about able people who refuse work in a church; not a rule against helping the sick or poor, and not a firing rule.
- Passages: 3:6-15.

### 1 Timothy (S054)
- Genre: pastoral letter.
- Teaches: provide for your household; godliness with contentment; the love of money is a root of evil; the laborer is worthy of wages; test people before trust; do not be partial.
- Seat use: A root of evil, not the root; the text is about the love of money. Qualification lists are for overseers.
- Passages: 1:19, 3:2-10, 5:8-22, 6:1-19.

### Titus (S056)
- Genre: pastoral letter.
- Teaches: leaders must be blameless, not greedy for dishonest gain; deeds must match words; submit to rulers; avoid foolish disputes; warn twice, then avoid.
- Seat use: Titus 2:9-10 and 3:1 are first-century household and civic instructions. Use for character in a manager.
- Passages: 1:5-16, 2:7-10, 3:1-14.

### Philemon (S057)
- Genre: short personal letter.
- Teaches: appeal rather than command; a changed person is received; put a pledge in writing; free-will goodness.
- Seat use: one private case. It does not say whether Onesimus was freed and is not a business rule on employment or debt.
- Passages: 8-22.

### Hebrews (S058)
- Genre: sermon-letter; chapter 13 is closing commands.
- Teaches: hospitality to strangers; be free from the love of money; do good and share; keep a good conscience; leaders give account.
- Seat use: not a ban on profit or a promise a business will not fail. Verse 17 is about church leaders.
- Passages: 13:1-18.

### James (S059)
- Genre: letter of direct commands to Jewish believers.
- Teaches: pay wages without fraud; no partiality by wealth; faith shows in help; plans held under God's will; plain yes and no; quick to hear, slow to anger.
- Seat use: James 5:1-6 targets defrauding landowners, not all wealth. Faith and works is read in different ways among Christians. Oath ban is debated.
- Passages: 1:5, 1:19-27, 2:1-17, 3:13-18, 4:1-17, 5:1-16.

### 1 Peter (S060)
- Genre: letter to scattered believers under Roman rule.
- Teaches: subject yourselves to human ordinance for the Lord's sake; do not repay evil for evil; hospitality without grumbling; lead without greed; cast your worries on God.
- Seat use: not a command to obey any law whatever; Acts shows refusals. Good managers in 4:10 means gifts, not property.
- Passages: 2:12-23, 3:8-16, 4:8-15, 5:1-8.
