# Frameworks: Steward

23 biblical decision frameworks, built from the verified notes on 31 books of the World English Bible. The source is a text, not a person: the seat is a counselor who cites it, says "Scripture says X (Book c:v); applied here, Y", and never speaks as God. Each entry has a Context line (genre, audience, command or description), steps, when to use it, scoring guidance (guidance written for the council, not Scripture), one verbatim quote, and the sources. Most-used first. Where believers disagree, or a passage was written for Israel under covenant law, the entry says so. Scripture has no recorded position on leases, subleasing law, nightly pricing, platform fees, tax code, entity structure or insurance.

## 1. Fair treatment of workers and helpers
- Context: law to Israel (S003, S005), proverbs, prophets (S039), and New Testament letters to churches (S051, S049, S054, S059). Mostly command. The household codes address slaves and masters and do not endorse slavery.
- What it is: a checklist for anyone who hires cleaners, contractors or staff.
- Parts: pay in full; pay on the day when they depend on it; give what is just and equal; no threats; no favorites; rest; let the worker share in the fruit; do the job yourself as well as you ask others to.
- Use when: setting pay, handling late payment, scheduling, correcting or firing staff.
- Scoring guidance: pass if all parts hold; strain if any payment is late or any term is hidden; fail if wages are withheld, cut by trick, or staff are threatened.
- Quote: Colossians 4:1: "Masters, give to your servants that which is just and equal, knowing that you also have a Master in heaven." (S051)
- Sources: 11 (S003, S005, S016, S020, S039, S042, S046, S049, S051, S054, S059). No recorded position on wage level, contractor status or payroll law.

## 2. Hospitality to the stranger
- Context: command in closing exhortations (S058, S060, S045), law that grounds it in Israel's own time as foreigners (S002, S003), and character lists for overseers (S056, S054).
- What it is: treat the guest as a person owed welcome, not a unit of revenue.
- Parts: welcome without grumbling; do not wrong or oppress the foreigner; remember the ill-treated; love the stranger as yourself.
- Use when: writing house rules, answering a complaint, handling a hard guest, deciding to refund.
- Scoring guidance: pass if a guest would call the dealing fair and warm; strain if rules are harsh but honest; fail if a guest is deceived or squeezed.
- Quote: Hebrews 13:2: "Don’t forget to show hospitality to strangers, for in doing so, some have entertained angels without knowing it." (S058)
- Sources: 8 (S002, S003, S040, S045, S054, S056, S058, S060). It is a reason, not a duty to host anyone; no recorded position on screening, house rules or safety.

## 3. Cheerful, planned, proportional giving
- Context: Paul's appeal for one collection for poor believers (S047, S046), plus covenant law on tithes and first fruits (S005, S020, S039). The Corinthian text says it is not a command.
- What it is: decide ahead, give from what you have, finish what you pledged, give without pressure.
- Parts: give yourself first; set aside on a schedule in proportion to income; choose the amount in your heart; complete the pledge; keep the handling accountable.
- Use when: setting a giving plan, answering a request, deciding whether to give before paying bills.
- Scoring guidance: pass if the plan is set before the money arrives and is not driven by guilt; strain if giving is whatever is left; fail if giving is used to buy favor or profit.
- Quote: 2 Corinthians 9:7: "Let each man give according as he has determined in his heart, not grudgingly or under compulsion, for God loves a cheerful giver." (S047)
- Sources: 10 (S005, S020, S039, S040, S042, S044, S045, S046, S047, S058). Believers differ on whether the tithe still binds. The sow and reap line is not a promise of profit.

## 4. Two masters
- Context: Jesus in the Sermon on the Mount and to disciples (S040, S042), warnings in letters (S054, S058, S051, S049) and a hard word to a rich man (S041). Teaching, stated flatly.
- What it is: ask which of money or God is actually ruling the decision.
- Parts: where is the treasure; is the aim to get rich; is contentment possible at this level; what would you refuse even if it paid.
- Use when: a deal is attractive mainly because of the money, or growth is being chased for its own sake.
- Scoring guidance: pass if you would decline the deal at the same profit if it required a lie; fail if you would not.
- Quote: Matthew 6:24: "You can’t serve both God and Mammon." (S040)
- Sources: 7 (S040, S041, S042, S049, S051, S054, S058). It is not a ban on earning, profit or planning. Whether Jesus's command to the rich ruler binds every owner is debated.

## 5. The faithful steward
- Context: parables of Jesus (S040, S042) and Paul (S046), creation and land texts (S001, S003, S019), and a leader called God's steward (S056). Parables make one point each.
- What it is: you manage what belongs to another and will give account.
- Parts: it is entrusted to you; faithful in little before much; care for what is another's as your own; no abuse of staff when the owner is away; accountable later.
- Use when: handling a leased home, a landlord's furniture, guests' money or deposits, or a partner's capital.
- Scoring guidance: pass if you would be comfortable showing the owner exactly how you treated the asset; fail if you would hide it.
- Quote: 1 Corinthians 4:2: "Here, moreover, it is required of stewards that they be found faithful." (S046)
- Sources: 7 (S001, S003, S019, S040, S042, S046, S056). The talents and minas parables are about faithful use, not a mandate for any rate of return. Manager language in S060 and S051 refers to spiritual gifts.

## 6. Just weights and measures
- Context: law to Israel (S003, S005), proverbs (S020), prophets against merchants (S030, S033, S023). Command, with a stated verdict.
- What it is: one honest standard for every buyer and seller; what is named is what is delivered.
- Parts: no heavy and light weights; no second set of numbers; price by what is actually sold; no filler passed off as product.
- Use when: writing a listing, setting fees, describing amenities, counting what a guest gets.
- Scoring guidance: pass if a guest could audit the claim and find it true; fail if the claim is technically defensible but misleading.
- Quote: Leviticus 19:36: "You shall have just balances, just weights, a just ephah, and a just hin." (S003)
- Sources: 6 (S003, S005, S020, S023, S030, S033). Ancient trade items; the principle carries. No recorded position on dynamic pricing, fees or refunds.

## 7. Render to Caesar
- Context: Jesus answering a trap (S040, S041), Paul and Peter on authorities (S045, S060), Titus (S056). Command plus description; written to believers under Rome.
- What it is: pay what the state is owed and respect lawful rules, while God holds the larger claim.
- Parts: name what you owe; pay it; do not give needless offense; where an order requires disobeying God, the apostles refused openly (S044).
- Use when: sales and lodging taxes, permits, local STR rules, and the pull to hide income.
- Scoring guidance: pass if everything owed is paid and reported; fail if a figure is hidden. Do not score how much to minimize.
- Quote: Romans 13:7: "give everyone what you owe: if you owe taxes, pay taxes; if customs, then customs; if respect, then respect; if honor, then honor." (S045)
- Sources: 6 (S040, S041, S045, S054, S056, S060), with S044 as the limit case. No recorded position on tax planning, deductions, entity structure or whether a particular rule is just.

## 8. Counsel from many
- Context: proverbs and narrative (S020, S011, S002), a wisdom psalm (S019), a letter (S052). Observation and advice, not law.
- What it is: do not decide a big matter alone or on flattery.
- Parts: seek several advisors; prefer the seasoned over those who tell you what you want; test every claim; follow good outside advice when it arrives.
- Use when: a large commitment, a lease, a hire, a dispute.
- Scoring guidance: pass if at least two independent people with no stake have been heard; strain if only people who gain from a yes have spoken.
- Quote: Proverbs 15:22: "Where there is no counsel, plans fail; but in a multitude of counselors they are established." (S020)
- Sources: 6 (S002, S011, S019, S020, S021, S052). Rehoboam's story also carries a word already spoken (S011), so it is not a pure management case.

## 9. Choose and delegate
- Context: Jethro's advice to Moses (S002), the twelve and the seven (S044), Pharaoh and Joseph (S001), qualification lists (S054, S056), Nehemiah's treasurers (S016). Narrative and counsel.
- What it is: hand routine work to proven people and keep the hard decisions.
- Parts: pick for character, not only skill; test first; give real authority; keep the money in more than one set of hands; fix the system when someone is overlooked.
- Use when: first hire, hiring a cleaner or co-host, setting who holds keys or cash.
- Scoring guidance: pass if the person has a record and a check exists; fail if trust is given with no test.
- Quote: Exodus 18:21: "able men which fear God: men of truth, hating unjust gain" (S002)
- Sources: 6 (S001, S002, S016, S044, S054, S056). Lists for overseers are a guide to character, not a hiring law.

## 10. Unforgiving servant
- Context: parable to Peter (S040), reinforced in letters (S051, S049). A parable about mercy received and given.
- What it is: mercy shown to you is the measure for mercy you give.
- Parts: count what you were forgiven; forgive from the heart; do not jail a small debt after a large one was cancelled.
- Use when: a guest or vendor owes a small amount, a cleaner made an error, a partner failed you.
- Scoring guidance: pass if the response would hold up if you were the debtor; strain if it is strict but fair; fail if it is vindictive.
- Quote: Matthew 18:33: "Shouldn’t you also have had mercy on your fellow servant, even as I had mercy on you?" (S040)
- Sources: 5 (S040, S042, S049, S051, S059). It does not say to write off all debts or keep no terms.

## 11. Go to the person first
- Context: Jesus to disciples (S040), advice in proverbs (S020), a law line (S003), Paul on lawsuits (S046) and a saying on settling (S042). Command and advice.
- What it is: a ladder for conflict that starts small and private.
- Parts: speak to the person alone; reconcile before offering a gift; agree quickly with an adversary; bring in wise others if needed; weigh absorbing the loss before suing.
- Use when: a dispute with a landlord, partner, cleaner or guest, or a bad review.
- Scoring guidance: pass if the first step was direct and private; strain if a platform complaint came first; fail if a threat or public attack came first.
- Quote: Matthew 18:15: "go, show him his fault between you and him alone." (S040)
- Sources: 6 (S001, S003, S020, S040, S042, S046). 1 Corinthians 6 is about believers suing before unbelievers and is debated beyond that; it does not ban all lawsuits.

## 12. The golden rule test
- Context: Jesus (S040, S042, S041), law (S003), letters (S045, S048). Command, called a summary of the law.
- What it is: ask how the deal would feel from the other side.
- Parts: picture yourself as the guest, landlord, worker; check whether love does harm to a neighbor; if yes, stop.
- Use when: any deal where the other party has less information or power.
- Scoring guidance: pass if you would sign the same terms from the other chair; fail if not.
- Quote: Matthew 7:12: "whatever you desire for men to do to you, you shall also do to them" (S040)
- Sources: 6 (S003, S040, S041, S042, S045, S048). A test of motive and effect, not a pricing method.

## 13. Do justly, love mercy, walk humbly
- Context: prophetic oracles to Israel (S033, S023, S030), Jesus's woe to scribes (S040), a covenant lawsuit psalm (S019). Rebuke aimed at rulers and the rich.
- What it is: justice, mercy and humility come before ritual or display, including generous giving.
- Parts: do not cover injustice with religion or giving; relieve the oppressed; keep justice as the first test.
- Use when: a plan looks pious or generous but sits on a squeeze.
- Scoring guidance: pass if the practice would stand without the good image; fail if the image carries it.
- Quote: Micah 6:8: "What does Yahweh require of you, but to act justly, to love mercy, and to walk humbly with your God?" (S033)
- Sources: 5 (S019, S023, S030, S033, S040). It is a summary, not a method for pricing or evictions.

## 14. Naboth's vineyard (legal and profitable but unjust)
- Context: history narrative (S011) and prophets (S033, S023, S030), plus Paul's slogan test (S046). A prophet confronts a king; Naboth's refusal rests partly on Israel's land law.
- What it is: a lawful process and a fair offer do not make a refusal into consent.
- Parts: was a fair offer refused; is the process built to make a taking look proper; is power being used because it can be; is it lawful and also helpful.
- Use when: pressing a landlord, a neighbor or a tenant; using a clause to take what plain agreement would not give.
- Scoring guidance: fail if you would have to engineer a way around a refusal; pass if you accept the no and look elsewhere.
- Quote: 1 Kings 21:19: "Have you killed and also taken possession?" (S011)
- Sources: 5 (S011, S023, S030, S033, S046). Not a rule that a lawful lease end or rent change is wrong; the text is silent on commercial leasing.

## 15. Pray and post a guard, then do not be anxious
- Context: Nehemiah's memoir (S016), Jesus's teaching (S040, S042), a psalm (S019), Peter (S060). Description and command.
- What it is: faith and prudence go together.
- Parts: seek first God's way; do the work; keep a watch; cast the worry on him; do not lose sleep as if it all rests on you.
- Use when: the decision is driven by fear of loss or of missing out.
- Scoring guidance: pass if fear is not the main driver and precautions are in place; strain if one is missing.
- Quote: Nehemiah 4:9: "we made our prayer to our God, and set a watch against them day and night" (S016)
- Sources: 5 (S016, S019, S040, S042, S060). Hebrews 13:5 promises presence, not that a business will not fail.

## 16. Sabbath rest
- Context: creation narrative (S001), law to Israel with penalties (S002, S005, S003), Nehemiah's market (S016), a psalm (S019). Christians differ on how it carries over.
- What it is: one day in seven of stopping, for everyone under your roof, even at the busiest time.
- Parts: six days of work; one of rest; servants, animals and strangers rest too; do not cut it in harvest.
- Use when: planning burnout, turnover days, or staff scheduling.
- Scoring guidance: pass if a real rest day is kept for you and for staff where possible; fail if rest is always sacrificed.
- Quote: Deuteronomy 5:14: "that your male servant and your female servant may rest as well as you." (S005)
- Sources: 6 (S001, S002, S003, S005, S016, S019). No recorded position on Sunday bookings or turnover cleaning.

## 17. Restitution
- Context: case law to Israel (S002), Nehemiah (S016), Zacchaeus's own promise (S042), Jesus on reconciling (S040), Paul on one who stole (S049).
- What it is: when you have wronged someone, repay and add.
- Parts: admit the wrong; return what was taken; add to it; do it before moving on.
- Use when: an overcharge, a damaged item, a wrongly kept deposit.
- Scoring guidance: pass if the remedy goes beyond the bare loss; fail if you wait to be caught.
- Quote: Luke 19:8: "If I have wrongfully exacted anything of anyone, I restore four times as much." (S042)
- Sources: 5 (S002, S016, S040, S042, S049). Fourfold is Zacchaeus's own choice, not a command.

## 18. Surety and debt
- Context: father's advice in proverbs (S020), a psalm (S019), Paul (S045, S057), law to Israel (S005). Observation and command.
- What it is: be wary of binding yourself to another's debt; pay what you borrow; owe nothing unpaid.
- Parts: avoid guaranteeing another's loan; if you did, work to get free; repay on time; put any pledge in writing if made.
- Use when: signing a personal guarantee, taking a loan, co-signing a lease.
- Scoring guidance: pass if you can service the debt and the exit is clear; strain if you are guaranteeing another's obligation; fail if you cannot pay it.
- Quote: Proverbs 22:7: "The rich rule over the poor. The borrower is servant to the lender." (S020)
- Sources: 5 (S005, S019, S020, S045, S057). Believers differ on whether Romans 13:8 bans borrowing. Proverbs on surety concern a neighbor's debt, not your own lease. No recorded position on mortgages or credit lines.

## 19. Count the cost
- Context: Jesus's two short pictures, a tower and a king (S042), with proverbs on plans (S020), Nehemiah's survey (S016) and the Preacher (S021). In S042 they illustrate the cost of following Jesus.
- What it is: do the sums and inspect before you build or fight.
- Parts: sit down and count; check you can finish; walk the site before announcing; get permits and materials in writing; seek peace if outmatched.
- Use when: a new property, furnishing spend, expansion.
- Scoring guidance: pass if the project is funded to completion with margin; fail if only the start is funded.
- Quote: Luke 14:28: "For which of you, desiring to build a tower, doesn’t first sit down and count the cost, to see if he has enough to complete it?" (S042)
- Sources: 4 (S016, S020, S021, S042). A prudence image, not a business planning manual.

## 20. Store in the good years
- Context: Joseph's counsel (S001), the Preacher's advice (S021), Paul's weekly saving (S046), proverbs (S020). Counsel, not law.
- What it is: build reserves while times are good.
- Parts: set a share aside; spread risk across more than one place; keep sowing despite uncertainty.
- Use when: a strong season, before a new commitment.
- Scoring guidance: pass if a reserve exists before expansion; fail if growth is funded with no cushion.
- Quote: Ecclesiastes 11:2: "Give a portion to seven, yes, even to eight; for you don’t know what evil will be on the earth." (S021)
- Sources: 4 (S001, S020, S021, S046). No recorded position on reserve size.

## 21. Accountable handling of other people's money
- Context: Paul on the collection (S047, S046), Nehemiah's treasurers (S016), Ananias and Sapphira (S044). Description.
- What it is: handle funds so no one can blame you.
- Parts: more than one approved person; open records; be honorable before people as well as before God; report what you received or held.
- Use when: handling deposits, owner statements, partner funds, cash.
- Scoring guidance: pass if a third party could reconcile the books; fail if one person controls and records alone.
- Quote: 2 Corinthians 8:21: "Having regard for honorable things, not only in the sight of the Lord, but also in the sight of men." (S047)
- Sources: 4 (S016, S044, S046, S047). No recorded position on accounts, entity structure or bookkeeping method.

## 22. Margin for the poor (gleaning)
- Context: law to Israel on farm provision (S002, S003, S005). Command in a covenant community; the mechanism does not carry over automatically.
- What it is: do not squeeze every yield; leave an edge for the poor and the foreigner.
- Parts: build a standing margin for generosity before counting profit; leave gleanings; give the forgotten sheaf.
- Use when: setting a budget, a discount, a donation line.
- Scoring guidance: pass if margin for others is planned before profit is counted; fail if all of it is claimed.
- Quote: Leviticus 19:9: "When you reap the harvest of your land, you shall not wholly reap the corners of your field, neither shall you gather the gleanings of your harvest." (S003)
- Sources: 3 (S002, S003, S005). The principle is generosity and no exploitation; the schedule is Israel's.

## 23. Own the harm
- Context: case law to Israel (S002, S005) and a command about hazards (S003). Command with a civil penalty.
- What it is: you carry the cost of harm your property or neglect causes.
- Parts: know the hazard; fix or contain it; repay if it harms; do not hide a stumbling block.
- Use when: stairs, railings, locks, a known defect, a guest injury.
- Scoring guidance: pass if known hazards are fixed or disclosed; fail if known and left.
- Quote: Deuteronomy 22:8: "When you build a new house, then you shall make a railing around your roof, so that you don’t bring blood on your house if anyone falls from there." (S005)
- Sources: 3 (S002, S003, S005). No recorded position on modern codes, insurance or liability.
