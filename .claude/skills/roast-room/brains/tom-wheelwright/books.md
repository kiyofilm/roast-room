# Books: Tom Wheelwright

Built from the 55 sources (podcasts and blog posts, 2018 to 2024); book text was not fetched. Summaries are in my own words and inferred from what he says about the books. Publication years are not stated in the sources unless noted.

**Tax law has changed since these sources (2018 to 2024).** The books are tied to the law they were written under. The second edition of Tax-Free Wealth was updated for the 2017 law (as of 2018-11-07, S006).

## Tax-Free Wealth (his main book; first edition year not stated in sources, second edition reflects the 2017 Tax Cuts and Jobs Act)
- **Core thesis:** The tax law is mostly incentives, so ordinary people can pay little or no tax by doing what it rewards: build a business, own real estate, produce energy, give. The title means wealth that is tax-free by getting to the professional investor quadrant (S001).
- **Key frameworks (my words):** incentives not loopholes; the five ways to save tax with deferral last; change your facts; depreciation as magic and cost segregation (chapter seven, S044); buy, borrow, die "201" for real estate (S036); how to choose a tax advisor (chapter 23, which he calls the most important chapter, S037); his advice to hand it to a preparer who must read it (S022, S050).
- **Taught in sources:** S001, S006, S008, S022, S024, S029, S030, S036, S037, S044, S050.
- **Why he wrote it:** so owners can ask informed questions, since the CPA can only tell you which facts to change (S044, S050).
- **Limits:** reading it is not enough without action (S008). A promotional frame: he says to take his words to your advisor and not as fact (S050).

## The Win-Win Wealth Strategy: 7 Investments the Government Will Pay You to Make (his newer book; promoted 2023 to 2024)
- **Core thesis:** The tax law pays you to make certain investments, so the same incentive logic applies in many countries. In S044 (2022-02-10) it was called Incentives: 7 Investments the Government Will Pay You To Make, forthcoming, and he said it covers 15 countries.
- **Key frameworks (my words):** the incentive map (business, real estate, energy and similar); partner with the government; seven categories not enumerated in the notes (no recorded detail on the seven).
- **Taught in sources:** S034 (2023-01-06), S035 (2024-01-11), S041 (2023-06-23), S044 (2022-02-10).
- **Limits:** only the episode pages promote it, no chapter content is in the sources.

## Rich Dad titles he contributes to (not his books; he is a contributor or the Rich Dad CPA)
- **Why Doctors Don't Get Rich (Tom Burns):** Tom contributed; about using teams and real estate to be free of a paycheck; he says it applies to anyone with a paycheck (S031).
- **Kiyosaki's books:** he cites Rich Dad Poor Dad, The Cashflow Quadrant and Before You Quit Your Job as starting resources (S026, S031) and says Rich Dad Poor Dad is "really a book on accounting" built on financial statements (S044). He uses the Cashflow Quadrant to explain tax rates by income type (S001, S044).

## Books he cites and recommends (not his)
- **Agility (Leo Tilman and Chuck Jacoby):** three questions: what business are we in, what is the environment, are we decisive. He plans to use them with his team (S025).
- **Good to Great (Jim Collins):** right people on the bus, in the right seats (S052).
- **Start Your Own Corporation (Garrett Sutton):** his favorite of Garrett's books (S009).
- **401(k)aos (Andy Tanner):** a 401(k) puts you in Wall Street with no control (S045).
- **The Obstacle is the Way:** a tax law change as an obstacle that shows the way (S003).
- **Outliers (Malcolm Gladwell):** the 10,000 hour idea, used for choosing one asset class (S047).
- **Big Potential (Shawn Achor):** the more specifically you picture your future, the likelier you reach it (S046).
- **The Top 10 Reasons the Rich Go Broke (John MacGregor):** promoted, with a Kiyosaki foreword (S051).
- **More Important Than Money (Kiyosaki and advisors):** about building your team (S044).
- **Entrusted (Andrew Howell and co-author):** appears as a guest's book; he did not describe it (S016).
