# Principles: Tom Wheelwright

Built from 55 sources (podcasts 2018 to 2024, two blog posts, one interview), read through five batches of verified notes. 26 decision rules, most-supported first. "Sources" is the number of distinct sources that state or apply the rule (ids listed). Confidence: high = 3 or more sources, medium = 2, low = 1.

**Tax law has changed since these sources (2018 to 2024).** Law claims carry "as of [source date]". Confirm every rate, threshold and deadline with a licensed advisor. **Gap:** no source covers a leased-home arbitrage business and none states a full short-term-rental hour test. Where that matters, this file says "no recorded position".

1. **Do what the law rewards: employ, house, produce, give.** The code pays for what the government wants, so find the overlap with what you want to do. "Anything that the government wants you to do, they're going to do it through a tax incentive." (S001) "The key to working with an advisor and understanding the system for reducing taxes is understanding what the government wants you to do and how to make this work within what you want to do" (S028) Sources: 20 (S001, S002, S004, S013, S015, S020, S021, S022, S024, S028, S029, S030, S032, S033, S037, S042, S043, S044, S045, S055). Confidence: high.

2. **Tax changes only when your facts change.** The advisor shows the path; you take the action. "I can't reduce your taxes. You're the only one who can reduce your taxes." (S044) "Unless we take the action, the plan is worthless." (S008) Sources: 11 (S008, S012, S019, S023, S024, S028, S038, S042, S044, S053, S055). Confidence: high.

3. **Stay inside the law, always.** Everything he teaches is legal, structured, documented. "Always want to do it legally." (S022) "There's nothing wrong with structuring your life so that you pay the least amount of taxes legally possible." (S055) Sources: 9 (S001, S010, S015, S022, S024, S028, S029, S038, S055). Confidence: high.

4. **No documentation, no deduction.** Record it when it happens. "had it been done contemporaneously, in other words, when they actually had the expense, I think we would have won 90% of it." (S008) "If it's not documented, it's not a deduction." (S021) Sources: 8 (S002, S008, S010, S021, S039, S040, S042, S053). Confidence: high.

5. **Never speak to the IRS yourself.** Your advisor does it, and the advisor must not be afraid of the government. "I will never speak to the IRS. I will never speak to the IRS. That is not your job." (S022) "If they're afraid of the government, you need a new team member." (S010) Sources: 6 (S010, S022, S024, S039, S043, S020). Confidence: high.

6. **Do not skip a legitimate deduction out of fear.** Fear of audit is how people lose benefits. "Why are you afraid of something that is specifically allowed under the law?" ## Tensions and slips the notes flagged (do not pick a side silently)

- **S corporation salary advice differs across episodes.** S021 (2019-09-13): no more than half of what you take out, based on his IRS experience, "not a statute." S023 (2019-10-24): minimize salary but keep it reasonable, meaning what you would pay someone else. S053 (2019-12-20): consider paying yourself wages so the 20% deduction wage limit can work. All three can be true for different income levels, but he never reconciles them. Inferred from S021, S023, S053.
- **Years as a CPA.** "almost 40 years" in S020 (2019-08-29); "35 years" in S022 (2019-09-26); "over 40 years" with real estate investors in S029 (2020-10-01). Do not state a number.
- **Real estate professional hours.** In S044 (2022-02-10) the transcript gives "150 hours" in one place and 750 later. The note author flags the first as a slip. He also gives 500 hours for material participation and says six other tests exist. No recorded position on which test an STR arbitrage operator should aim for.
- **Short-term rentals and time.** S034 (2023-01-06): you can spend less time and still get the deductions, no hours or stay test named, and he says he is learning too. S044 (2022-02-10): seven days or less is a business, test material participation at 500 hours. Read together: the more careful statement is the one with the seven-day rule and the 500 hours, but he never ties the two together. Inferred from S034, S044.
- **Planning versus year end.** S012 says year end is the worst time to plan; S053 and S055 give year-end moves. Read as: plan all year, use December for leftovers. Inferred from S012, S053, S055.
- **Tax benefits as a reason.** S018 says "We never focus on capital gains or tax benefits" and then ranks tax benefits second. S013 says tax benefits get you to look, never to buy. Inferred from S013, S018.
- **Deductibility.** He used to say "most things are deductible, some aren't"; after a Kiyosaki challenge he says everything is possibly deductible with the right facts (S037). Read it as the more aggressive of the two; test with an advisor.
- **How cautious to be.** In S041 (2023-06-23) a guest advised erring toward safety in tax planning; he said "not music to my ear" and did not endorse it. Elsewhere he says to dot your I's and cross your T's (S015, S016, S021) and that aggressive means acting beyond your education (S044). Inferred from S015, S041, S044: his caution is about documentation and knowledge, not about using the incentive.
- **Bonus depreciation.** The percentage differs by date: 100% on contents and land improvements as of 2022-02-10 (S044), 80% and 60% as of 2024-01-11 (S035), about 30% of purchase as of 2018-08-20 (S036), up to $150,000 on $100,000 as of 2019-03-06 (S011, his claim as heard). All stale.
- **Estate exemption.** $22 million (S036, 2018-08-20), $22.8 million (S016, 2019-06-19), "20 some odd million" (S027, 2020-01-17). All stale; the law was set to change.
- **Unverified figures.** Some of his claims (80% of mutual fund earnings go to the fund, average 529 return, six times more likely to be disabled than die, 99% of whole life collected) are unsourced figures flagged by the notes as unverified (S045, S027, S049). Do not repeat them as fact.
