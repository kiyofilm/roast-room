# Principles: Danny Meyer

Built from the batch notes on six unique sources (S001 to S004, S006, S007; S005 is the same episode as S001 and counts once). 15 rules, most used first. Confidence: high = 3+ sources, medium = 2, low = 1. All come from restaurants, not rentals. Where no position exists on guests, reviews or complaints, the entry says so.

1. **Judge hospitality by how the guest felt, not by technical delivery.**
   - "it has everything to do with how you made the recipient of your product feel." (S002)
   - "it was how we were making people feel, not the technical service, but the emotional hospitality." (S007)
   - Sources: 4 (S001, S002, S003, S007). Confidence: high.

2. **Make the guest feel you are on their side.**
   - "It was did you make someone feel like you were on their side?" (S002)
   - "one of the first things that I would gauge when I go to any restaurant is do I feel at home here" (S006)
   - Sources: 3 (S001, S002, S006). Confidence: high.

3. **Look after your own people first. They carry it to the guest.**
   - "we actually put our employees first." (S003)
   - "their performance will be a direct result of how happy they are working there" (S006)
   - Sources: 4 (S001, S003, S006, S007). Confidence: high.

4. **Hire for emotional skill and heart, then train the technical part.**
   - "What you do have to have is a heart for hospitality, a work ethic, and a desire to grow." (S003)
   - Sources: 3 (S001, S002, S003). Confidence: high.

5. **Basics must work. Kindness does not rescue a broken promise.**
   - "If I don’t do the technical stuff right, the food sucks or it took too long, doesn’t matter how nice I am." (S001)
   - "you'd better do those things really well because they're not coming back if it's broken." (S003)
   - Sources: 2 (S001, S003). Confidence: medium.

6. **Treat a complaint as a gift and own the fix.** No recorded position on a refund or compensation policy.
   - "I gave you the gift, you go deal with it." (S001, said by Seth Godin; Meyer's response was to write a better next chapter for the guest)
   - "it almost takes someone saying you're not measuring up for us to kick into a higher gear" (S007)
   - Sources: 3 (S001, S006, S007). Confidence: high.

7. **Pay attention to reviews, but filter the noise.** He treats word of mouth as now living on review sites.
   - "we used to call it Word of Mouth now it's word of Yelp or word of Twitter" (S006)
   - "tune in the constructive stuff and tune out the stuff that's just hurtful" (S006)
   - Sources: 2 (S006, S004). In S004 a review calling the staff's niceness fake hurt him most (paraphrase). Confidence: medium. The 2012 S006 text is auto-captioned.

8. **Know the guest before they arrive and remember what they like.**
   - "I end every single night reading all the reservation reports for all of our restaurants so I know who’s dining in our restaurants tomorrow." (S001)
   - "they've got eight toppings, and I wanted to prove that we could remember" (S007)
   - Sources: 3 (S001, S003, S007). Confidence: high. Depends on face-to-face service; the rental version is inferred.

9. **Do not tolerate behavior that goes against the culture. It cancels the praise.**
   - "if I’m tolerating behaviors that don’t promote either the excellence or wellbeing of the team, then everything I’ve done on the positive side should be called into question." (S001)
   - Sources: 2 (S001, S002). Confidence: medium.

10. **Never let a firing be the first time the person hears about the problem.**
    - "it shouldn’t be the first time you’ve had the conversation." (S001)
    - Sources: 1 (S001). Confidence: low.

11. **Coach people who are willing for a long time. Move unwilling people out fast.**
    - "you’re going to have a loyal employee for life because you stuck with them." (S001)
    - Sources: 1 (S001). Confidence: low. He admits he was slow to fire and fired badly at first (S001).

12. **Remove surprises from the guest's bill.**
    - "So the menu price covers everything." (S003)
    - Sources: 2 (S002, S003). Confidence: medium. He reversed the no-tip model in 2020 (S001), so the pricing mechanics are not settled.

13. **Hospitality is the thing rivals cannot copy.**
    - "the thing that's going to convince someone that they don't have a choice but to come back, is the hospitality." (S003)
    - "it's the one thing you cannot take a photo of and plagiarize." (S002)
    - Sources: 2 (S002, S003). Confidence: medium.

14. **Go slowly and learn the business before you expand.**
    - "there’s a benefit to being a little bit patient and try to grow where you’re planted." (S001)
    - "we didn't open a second one for five years" (S007)
    - Sources: 3 (S001, S004, S007). In S004 he calls going from one to two among the hardest leaps of his career. Confidence: high.

15. **When you cannot be at the door, name the standard and teach it.** Standards slipped in 1995 when he was not at the front door (S007).
    - "once you can name something, then you can teach it, and then you can be intentional about it." (S002)
    - Sources: 2 (S002, S007). Confidence: medium.

16. **Start from a charitable assumption about the other person.**
    - "Can’t we please have a charitable assumption about one another?" (S001)
    - Sources: 1 (S001). Confidence: low.

## Where there is no recorded position
- Pricing a guest stay against competitors, discounting, refunds, damage deposits and house rules: no recorded position.
- Bad guests and dealing with abusive guests: no recorded position beyond "do not blame guests" in his style (inferred from S001, S004).
- Using automation and templated messages: he says technology should take transaction work off staff so they can focus on welcome (S003), but no recorded position on automated messages for remote guests.
