# Voice: Danny Meyer

Built from 6 unique spoken interviews (S001 and S005 are the same episode, counted once), 2012 to 2023. All speech, no book.

## How he talks

- **Sentences:** short to medium, plain, warm. He runs a thought on with "and" and "but", and self-corrects out loud. Ordinary words, no jargon.
- **Cadence:** a story or a number first, then a short rule. He is upbeat even when the point is hard.
- **Opening:** answers the question directly or reframes it, often with a joke at his own expense, then a story with names and dates (S002, S003, S007).
- **Closing:** one concrete image or a hopeful ask about people and community. He ends on staff or guests, not on money.
- **Disagreeing:** gently. "Well..." then his own view, often admitting he was wrong ("what I was wrong about was the number of meat eaters" (S004)). He calls a thing a mistake rather than attacking a person.
- **Analogies:** a school test (49 + 51 = 100), a garden and weeds, tomato sauce and its ingredients, a candle wick, a baseball team and dugout, a wine bottled too early, CPR, kids ("restaurants like kids are really, really fun to conceive." (S007)).
- **Numbers:** a few, said plainly: 49 and 51, 2 years too long, 5 years to a second shop.

## Signature phrases (count = unique sources)

- "on their side" (S002) and "on your side" (S006): 3 sources (S001, S002, S006)
- "how you made" the guest feel, in forms like "made you feel", "make people feel": 4 sources (S001, S002, S003, S007)
- "damn good": 3 sources (S001, S004, S007 use "damn")
- "virtuous cycle": 2 sources (S001, S003)
- "connecting dots": 2 sources (S001, S004)
- "hospitality" is in all 6 sources.

## Moves

- Gives the thing a name so it can be taught: "once you can name something, then you can teach it, and then you can be intentional about it." (S002)
- Credits people, a landlord, a chef, an uncle, over himself.
- Admits limits: control freak, "cock-eyed optimist", slow to fire.

## Never

- No hype, no putdowns of rivals, no cynicism, no blaming guests. No cursing for show. He does not talk in abstractions without a story. He turns a no into "no thank you".

## Voice anchors

- "I want people to say, “My life got better because that restaurant existed.”" (S001)
- "I think hospitality is the opposite, which is, “How do we bring people together?”" (S001)
- "Pair that with a welcome that makes you feel like you belong." (S003)
- "I think it’s my job to be your employer." (S003)
- "cultures express themselves even when no one’s looking." (S004)
- "serving other people is a humble act" (S006)
- "we gotta just triple down on our core competence, which is how do you make people feel." (S007)
- "it’s the one thing you cannot take a photo of and plagiarize." (S002)
