# Positions: Danny Meyer

Built from 6 unique spoken interviews (S001 and S005 are the same Tim Ferriss episode, counted once), 2012 to 2023. No book text. Dates are source dates (S004 is March 2020). Every source is restaurants, so each entry notes where the lesson leans on a staffed room. Topics with no evidence say so.

## 1. Guests and how they feel
- **Stance:** Hospitality is how the guest felt, not the technical delivery. The food has to be good first, then the guest must feel you are on their side. Service and product earn about 49 of 100, feeling earns 51.
- "hospitality has nothing to do with the technical delivery of the product, it has everything to do with how you made the recipient of your product feel." (S002, 2016-12)
- "the food better be damn good. But more than anything else, you’ve got to feel like we’re on your side." (S001, 2023-04)
- "If I don’t do the technical stuff right, the food sucks or it took too long, doesn’t matter how nice I am." (S001, 2023-04)
- **Restaurant note:** the 49/51 split is his own heuristic, not measured (S002). For a rental the same idea shows up in setup, message tone and recovery (inferred from S002, S003).

## 2. Knowing and remembering guests
- **Stance:** Collect facts about guests and act on them.
- "Always be collecting dots so you can always be connecting dots." (S001, 2023-04)
- "they’ve got eight toppings, and I wanted to prove that we could remember" (S007, 2018-11)
- **Restaurant note:** he reads tomorrow's reservation report and seats people with a host. A remote host has no room to do this in.

## 3. Reviews and criticism
- **Stance:** Pay attention, filter the noise, use criticism as fuel. Word of mouth now lives on review sites.
- "tune in the constructive stuff and tune out the stuff that’s just hurtful" (S006, 2012-04)
- "we used to call it Word of Mouth now it’s word of Yelp or word of Twitter" (S006, 2012-04)
- "it almost takes someone saying you’re not measuring up for us to kick into a higher gear" (S007, 2018-11)

## 4. Complaints from guests
- **Stance:** A complaint is a gift, and the owner owns the fix. Only one story backs this, told secondhand (Seth Godin).
- "I gave you the gift, you go deal with it." (S001, 2023-04, said by Seth Godin)
- No other recorded position on complaint handling steps.

## 5. Handling mistakes
- **Stance:** After a miss, think of what comes next for the guest.
- "I’ve got to write a great next chapter on this." (S001, 2023-04)
- When standards slipped at Gramercy Tavern in 1995, he named hospitality for the whole staff (S007, 2018-11, paraphrase).

## 6. Pricing and value, and no tipping
- **Stance:** The menu price covers everything, so the guest has no surprise and pays for how they felt. He later reversed the no-tip model.
- "So the menu price covers everything." (S003, 2019-07)
- "you’re basically paying me how I made you feel." (S003, 2019-07)
- **Over time:** 2019-07, "wouldn’t go back" (S003). 2023-04, he went back to tipping in summer 2020 and pays cooks a share of revenue: "this is inhumane, this is not being on our employees’ side." (S001)
- **Restaurant note:** the model split servers from cooks under a tip law. It does not map to a cleaner or host.

## 7. Hiring
- **Stance:** Hire for emotional skill and attitude first, then teach the craft. Six traits are named in S001: kind eyes, curiosity, work ethic, empathy, self-awareness, integrity (he would add "loves to win"). S002 does not list them.
- "I want kind optimists." (S001, 2023-04)
- "What you do have to have is a heart for hospitality, a work ethic, and a desire to grow." (S003, 2019-07)
- "to not only seek people who’ve got a lotta talent, but who at this point in their career actually has something to prove" (S007, 2018-11)

## 8. Training and coaching
- **Stance:** Coach willing people a long time, move unwilling people out fast.
- "the wick on my candle is pretty long for someone who will." (S001, 2023-04)
- "you’re going to have a loyal employee for life because you stuck with them." (S001, 2023-04)

## 9. Paying and treating staff
- **Stance:** Employees come first. Treat them as volunteers who could work anywhere, and care for all of them, not part.
- "we actually put our employees first." (S003, 2019-07)
- "I better give you a higher purpose and reason for wanting to be here." (S001, 2023-04)
- "it’s unconscionable to say in our culture we care for our employees first, but we only care for part of our employees." (S002, 2016-12)
- Cost: "the downward pressure on your pricing ability is great." (S004, 2020-03)

## 10. Firing and standards
- **Stance:** A firing should never be the first talk. Tolerated bad behavior cancels the praise. An integrity breach is a different, immediate conversation.
- "it shouldn’t be the first time you’ve had the conversation." (S001, 2023-04)
- He admits he was slow to fire and fired badly at first (S001).

## 11. Culture
- **Stance:** Culture is what you celebrate minus what you tolerate. It shows when nobody is looking.
- "the culture you have in your organization is the sum of all the wanted behaviors that you celebrate, minus all the unwanted behaviors that you tolerate." (S001, 2023-04)
- "cultures express themselves even when no one’s looking." (S004, 2020-03)
- "it’s a business, not a family." (S001, 2023-04)

## 12. Scaling
- **Stance:** Go slow, learn the business, keep the keys to your culture, stay picky.
- "That was amongst the hardest leaps in my entire career, going from one to two" (S004, 2020-03)
- "we just haven’t wanted to give the keys to our culture to anybody else." (S003, 2019-07)
- "if ever we get to the point where we are growing so much or so fast that we stop being picky, then we’re just like everybody else." (S003, 2019-07)
- **Restaurant note:** Shake Shack is company-owned in the US because the host is in the room. Standards must be written down when the owner cannot be at the door (inferred from S007).

## 13. Saying no
- **Stance:** A warm, gracious decline is owed to anyone who asked. Trouble comes from the shoulds.
- "If somebody had the courtesy to invite me to do something, they deserve the courtesy of a gracious response." (S001, 2023-04)
- "I’ve gotten into most trouble on the shoulds." (S001, 2023-04)

## 14. Saying yes
- **Stance:** Say yes to what you would be excited to do tonight, and to things that made your own life better. Then make the yes live up to the promise.
- "if this thing were tonight, is this something I would be excited to do or would I roll my eyes" (S001, 2023-04)
- "when we say yes, my job is to make sure that they live up to the promise of excellence in hospitality" (S003, 2019-07)

## 15. Growth and focus
- **Stance:** One filter across ventures: hospitality. Do not open two at once if you can help it.
- "there’s really only one headline, and that’s hospitality." (S003, 2019-07)
- "if I had really thought it out I never would have done two restaurants at the same time" (S007, 2018-11)
- "there’s a benefit to being a little bit patient and try to grow where you’re planted." (S001, 2023-04)

## 16. Competition
- **Stance:** Ideas get copied at once, so the moat is how people felt. He also copies and adapts others.
- "You can photograph how I cook my chicken, but you can’t photograph how the server made you feel when she was delivering it." (S002, 2016-12)
- "it’s the one thing you cannot take a photo of and plagiarize." (S002, 2016-12)

## 17. Brand
- **Stance:** The brand promise is great food and leaving feeling better. Marketing is dialogue and belonging.
- "our brand promise is that you expect the food to be great, but mostly you expect to feel better emotionally when you leave that experience." (S003, 2019-07)
- "great marketing actually doesn’t just sell you something. It makes you feel like you belong to a tribe." (S001, 2023-04)

## 18. Technology
- **Stance:** Technology should take transaction work off people so they can do hospitality. High tech needs more high touch.
- "human beings need more high touch the more high tech we get." (S003, 2019-07)
- **Restaurant note:** his kiosk and app freed on-site staff. For a rental the mapping is self check-in and automated messages (inferred from S003).

## 19. Partners and investors
- **Stance:** Investors are an outcome of a virtuous cycle, not the input. Use partners for what you do not know. He had a friend who left the plan early (S001, paraphrase).
- "It’s truly a virtuous cycle." (S003, 2019-07)
- "internationally, all of our relationships are licensed relationships." (S003, 2019-07)

## 20. Time
- **Stance:** Do the thing you would regret not doing. Give new places time to settle.
- "You’re going to be dead a hell of a lot longer than you’re going to be alive." (S001, 2023-04, said by his uncle)
- "it takes a while for the people who work in it to learn how to use it, and it takes a while for the guests to learn how to use it." (S002, 2016-12)
- **Restaurant note:** a rental is reviewed from the first stay, so this grace may not exist (flag).

## 21. Setbacks and closures
- **Stance:** Closing is not failure. The only real failure is not trying.
- "closing a restaurant is not failing, and it’s also nothing to be ashamed of and nothing to be proud of to keep a restaurant open forever." (S001, 2023-04)
- "I kept it open for two years longer than I should have." (S001, 2023-04)
- **Disagreement:** Tabla closed in 2010 (S002), "Tabla did go out of business in 2011." (S007). S006 (2012-04, auto-captioned) says "in 24 years I’ve never had the experience of closing a restaurant" (S006). Closures: one named in 2016 (S002), "we’ve closed four in 34 years, almost 34 years." (S003, 2019-07).

## 22. Community
- **Stance:** Invest in your community because it is right and good business.
- "it’s reason enough to invest in your community because it’s the right thing to do but also that it’s good business to do that" (S006, 2012-04)
- "why should your community root for your success if you’re not investing in your community?" (S001, 2023-04)

## 23. COVID
- One brief remark, March 2020, no plans or numbers: "coronavirus is certainly very different than 9/11" (S004). After 9/11 his first step was to address fear, then the economy (S004, inferred).

## 24. Not recorded
- Delegating to hired help and cleaners, cleanliness standards, refunds and discounts to guests, and online reviews of a home: no recorded position.
